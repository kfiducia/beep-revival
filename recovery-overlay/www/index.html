🛟 Beep Recovery

This speaker booted into its recovery firmware because the main firmware failed to start. Recovery has no audio — its only job is to get a known-good firmware back on the device over the network. Nothing is wrong with the hardware.

How to restore:
  1. Download the latest signed Beep release .bin.
  2. Upload it below. Recovery verifies its signature against the key baked into this device, then flashes it and reboots.
  3. Only genuine, signed releases are accepted — an unsigned or tampered image is rejected before it can touch the flash.

The device will reboot into the restored firmware automatically. Keep the power on.
